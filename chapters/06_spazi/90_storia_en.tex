\section{Historical Notes}

\label{note:Basilea}%
The Basel problem is a problem posed by Mengoli in 1650 and solved by Euler in
1734 after the leading mathematicians of the time (including the famous members
of the Bernoulli family who lived in Basel) had attempted in vain to solve it.
The problem involves determining the sum of the series \eqref{eq:basilea}.
Euler's solution (completely different from the one we are proposing here) was
later revisited by Riemann, who defined the celebrated \emph{zeta} function
\index{Riemann!$\zeta$ function}%
\index{$\zeta$!of Riemann}%
\index{Basel!problem of}%
\index{problem!of Basel}%
\index{Euler!Basel problem}%
\index{$\pi$!Basel problem}%
\[
\zeta(s) = \sum_{k=1}^{+\infty} \frac{1}{k^s}  
\]
which is of great significance in number theory.
